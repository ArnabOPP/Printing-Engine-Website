# Printing Engine

## Register

product

## Users

Printing Engine staff manage orders, customer artwork, production and delivery. Customers use the separate storefront to buy ready-made products and customise printed items.

## Product Purpose

Help staff review the exact custom design and options ordered, move orders through production, and manage delivery without losing artwork or duplicating order records.

## Brand Personality

Practical, direct and clear. Preserve the existing dark admin interface and lime action colour, as confirmed by the user for the Custom Orders page.

## Anti-references

Avoid introducing marketing-style layouts, decorative animations, new visual themes or duplicated shipping workflows into the admin panel.

## Design Principles

- Show artwork, mockups and exact production specifications together.
- Keep one order record and one shipment even when the order appears in multiple admin views.
- Preserve familiar controls, filters, navigation and permissions.
- Explain empty, loading, removed-file and failure states clearly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep controls keyboard accessible, use explicit labels and visible focus states, and retain usable mobile layouts. Avoid introducing motion or colour-only status information.
